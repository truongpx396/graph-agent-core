# Lead Reply Draft

1. Call `search_docs` for what the lead asked about.
2. Write the reply using only what came back. If the knowledge base says nothing about a price, a plan, a feature or a date they asked about, do not supply one: the draft says a colleague will confirm it.
3. Keep it short: a greeting, the answer, one clear next step, and a sign-off left blank for the rep.
4. Under the draft, list "To confirm before sending:" with every point the knowledge base did not cover.
5. Call it a draft. Nothing here sends it: a person reviews and sends it.
